# Project £1

The world's most honest business.

## About

Project €1 is an open-source internet experiment exploring whether a simple, humorous idea can create community participation.
People donate a simple €1 to me and recieve absolutley nothing in return. They fund my ridiculous antics all to see how far crowdfunding can truly go. 

Its time we the people stand up to these big corporations - you give them money and they give negative impacts on your lives. 
Instead donate to me - You give me money and I dont impact your life in the slightest, its a win win for everyone.
